Assumptions & methodology

Compares Year 1 and recurring costs assuming both Buy and Build meet your security, data-handling, and integration requirements. Uses 50 working weeks (250 working days) per year; enter 0 only when confirmed zero. As an illustrative reference (not an industry benchmark), one real-world CI setup saw about $2 per review run on frontier models (~$4 per PR at ~2 runs, rough estimate, not measured), with 1–2 days/week maintenance in the first 2 months and ~0.5 day/week after that.
